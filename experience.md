# Work Experience

## Research Intern — Lucas TVS  
**May 2022 – Jun 2022 | Chennai, India**

At **Lucas TVS**, I worked closely with both the **production and R&D teams**, gaining hands-on experience in automotive manufacturing and product innovation. My responsibilities included monitoring large-scale production workflows and assisting with research initiatives.

**Key contributions:**

- Supervised assembly lines for **alternators, starters, motors, and wipers**, gaining insight into mass production processes and workflow optimization.
- Collaborated with the **R&D department** to explore new product innovations and evaluate emerging technologies for future automotive applications.
- Developed an understanding of how **manufacturing processes intersect with research and product development**, bridging practical assembly-line practices with engineering innovation.

This internship provided valuable industry exposure in **production engineering, process optimization, and applied research**, complementing my academic work in robotics and automation.

**Jun 2022 – Jul 2022 | Chennai, India**

During this internship, I worked with the engineering team at **WIKA Group**, focusing on the **design and production of industrial sensors** used for flow, temperature, and pressure measurement. I gained exposure to the full lifecycle of industrial instrumentation—from conceptual design to final calibration.

**Key contributions:**

- Supported the **design and prototyping** of precision measurement devices used in industrial sensing applications.
- Assisted in **sensor calibration procedures**, ensuring accuracy and compliance with industry standards.
- Learned about **quality control practices** and the importance of maintaining reliability in industrial instrumentation systems.

This experience strengthened my understanding of **sensor technologies and measurement systems**, knowledge that continues to influence my work in **robot perception and sensing systems**.
